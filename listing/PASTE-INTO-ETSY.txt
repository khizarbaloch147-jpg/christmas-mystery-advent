Description
A 24-day Christmas mystery for one player. The star is gone from the Harrington house on the evening of 23 December 2026. Open one door, solve one puzzle, and keep every clue in the notebook. On the last door, name who took the star, why, and where it is hidden.

Advent opens door 1 on 1 December, door 2 on 2 December, and so on. After 24 December every door is open. Before December the doors stay shut. Binge opens the next door as soon as the current puzzle is solved, on any date.
